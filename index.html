Personal desktop application

Calendar context and study planning, under the Owner's control.

Jarvis Calendar connects a person's selected Google Calendar to their local Jarvis assistant. It helps the Owner review upcoming commitments, find available study time, and prepare calendar changes.

What the application does

  • Shows calendar context alongside assignments and local morning and evening digests.
  • Proposes study blocks within the Owner's editable weekly schedule.
  • Creates private timed events, or updates supported Jarvis events, only after the Owner approves the exact change in the local application.

This is a private, single-owner application. This website explains its Google Calendar connection. It does not provide a hosted Jarvis service, an account signup, or access to the Owner's calendar.

Google Calendar access

Google consent permits viewing and editing calendar events and reading the calendar list. Jarvis verifies the selected calendar's identity, ownership and timezone. Its trusted local code limits writes to that selected calendar and separately approved actions. Connecting Google alone does not authorize an event change.

Privacy and support

Read the privacy policy for data access, local storage, optional model use, revocation and deletion. The terms of use explain this personal installation.

Support is provided by the application's developer and Owner through the support email displayed on the Google consent screen. Do not send passwords, OAuth files or calendar contents through public channels.
